## Publication Status

Papers may be in different stages of the research and publication process:

- **Draft**: Work in progress.
- **Preprint**: Publicly available manuscript that may not have undergone peer review.
- **Under Review**: Submitted to a journal or conference and awaiting a decision.
- **Published**: Formally published in a journal, conference, or other scholarly venue.

Each paper's status should reflect its actual publication stage.
